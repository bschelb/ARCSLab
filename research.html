Our Research

We use rigorous mixed-methods inquiry to understand and improve how humans and AI systems collaborate — building the scientific foundation for trustworthy, high-performing human-AI teams across mission-critical domains.

01
🤝
Core Focus
Human-AI Teaming

Human-AI teaming sits at the intersection of human factors, cognitive systems engineering, and AI. We study how humans and AI agents develop shared understanding, coordinate actions, and sustain effective collaboration — particularly in dynamic, high-stakes environments where mistakes are costly.

We examine team cognition constructs including shared mental models, transactive memory systems, and situational awareness within mixed human-AI teams, using both controlled laboratory experiments and real-world applied contexts.

Modeling information-sharing behaviors and perceived information needs in human-AI teams
Examining team composition effects: how the presence of AI teammates changes team dynamics
Designing AI teammates that adapt to human cognition and workload in real time
Investigating transition phases and role shifts when AI autonomy levels change
Studying ad hoc team formation and performance in collaborative technology environments

Core Focus
Trustworthy AI

Trust is the linchpin of effective human-AI collaboration. Poorly calibrated trust — both overtrust and undertrust — undermines safety and performance. Our research investigates the multidimensional nature of trust: how it forms, breaks, spreads across team networks, and can be repaired.

We also study the ethical dimensions of AI behavior: how AI decision-making is perceived through a moral lens, how ethical violations affect team trust, and what design principles promote responsible AI that humans can rely on appropriately.

Empirically investigating the relationship between AI ethics and trust in human-AI teaming
Studying how trust spreads — and distrust propagates — across human-AI team networks
Examining how team role and violation type shape trust after AI ethical failures
Designing explainable AI behaviors that support accurate trust calibration
Developing frameworks for trust repair following AI performance or ethical violations

Applied Focus
Training

Effective training is essential for humans operating alongside AI in complex environments. Our research explores how to design experiences that maximize skill transference, build appropriate mental models of AI capabilities, and prepare human operators for the realities of joint human-AI work.

Comparing independent vs. collaborative training approaches and their effects on human-AI team performance
Developing intelligent training interventions that adapt to individual and team performance states
Enhancing psychological fidelity in simulation-based training environments
Using generative AI to create lightweight, scalable simulations for training future autonomous teammates

Core Focus
Situational Awareness

Situational awareness (SA) — the perception, comprehension, and projection of relevant environmental elements — is critical for effective decision-making in complex human-AI teams. Our work investigates how AI teammates can enhance or degrade SA, and how interface design shapes collective understanding in contested environments.

Examining the role of spatial information and AI-shared cues in human-agent team situational awareness
Developing adaptive interfaces that surface mission-critical information for human operators
Studying shared SA in electronically contested / EW environments (current ARL project)
Modeling how shared mental model accuracy relates to team SA and performance outcomes

Applied Focus
Applied Robotics

Autonomous and semi-autonomous robotic systems are increasingly integrated into manufacturing, logistics, and emergency response. Our lab examines how to design and deploy collaborative robots and UGVs so they function as true teammates — safe, predictable, and effective partners for human operators.

Human-robot teaming in advanced manufacturing: ergonomics, workload, and trust
Designing unmanned ground vehicles (UGVs) for search-and-rescue in dynamic environments
Adaptive autonomy frameworks for robotic systems operating in high-risk contexts
Evaluating robot transparency and human acceptance of robotic AI teammates

Evaluation & Validation

Rigorous evaluation is foundational to ARCS Lab work. We develop and apply experimental frameworks — drawing on quantitative, qualitative, and computational methods — to robustly test AI teammate effectiveness, safety, and resilience before deployment in real-world settings.

Designing and validating game-theoretic and reinforcement learning models of human-AI cooperation
Mixed-methods approaches combining surveys, behavioral measures, and verbal protocols
Developing testbeds for evaluating AI teammate behavior under varied autonomy and risk conditions
Addressing vulnerability identification and mitigation for compromised AI teammates (ARL project)

Methodology

How We Work

ARCS Lab research is fundamentally interdisciplinary and mixed-methods, combining the rigor of experimental science with the depth of qualitative inquiry.

📊
Quantitative

Controlled experiments, surveys, behavioral measurement, structural equation modeling, network analysis, and statistical inference to test causal hypotheses about human-AI interaction.

💬
Qualitative

Thematic analysis, think-aloud protocols, semi-structured interviews, and expert elicitation to contextualize findings and surface constructs that resist quantification.

⚙️
Computational

Reinforcement learning models, game-theoretic frameworks, agent-based simulation, and generative AI tools to build and test formal models of human-AI collaborative behavior.

Applied Domains

Where We Work

Our research addresses real-world challenges across high-stakes domains where human-AI teaming has the greatest potential impact.

⚔️
Defense & Command

Shared situational awareness in electronically contested environments, AI on the battlefield, and compromised AI mitigation for warfighters.

🏭
Advanced Manufacturing

Collaborative robotics integration, human-cobot workload distribution, and trust in automated manufacturing workflows.

🏥
Healthcare

Ethical AI decision support in clinical settings, adaptive autonomy for healthcare AI teammates, and human factors in medical AI deployment.

🆘
Emergency Response

Human-robot teaming in search-and-rescue, AI decision support for first responders, and team cognition under high-stress conditions.

🔐
Cybersecurity

Human behavior modeling in cybersecurity contexts, cognitive biases in security decision-making, and AI-assisted defense strategies.

💼
Future of Work

AI teammate integration in organizational contexts, workforce augmentation, and the human dimensions of AI-enabled productivity.

⚖️
Responsible AI

AI ethics in practice, responsible deployment frameworks, and the societal implications of human-AI teaming at scale.

🧠
Decision-Making

Human-AI complementarity in complex decisions, cognitive bias mitigation, and advice-taking from AI systems under uncertainty.
