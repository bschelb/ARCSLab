Dr. Beau G.
Schelble
Assistant Professor, Industrial & Systems Engineering
Founding Director, AI & Robotics for Collaborative Systems Lab
Tickle College of Engineering · University of Tennessee, Knoxville
Dr. Schelble is an interdisciplinary researcher whose work sits at the intersection of human factors, human-centered computing, and AI. For nearly a decade he has explored how humans and AI can work together more effectively and safely — investigating trust, shared mental models, situational awareness, ethical AI behavior, and intelligent information sharing. His research spans defense, advanced manufacturing, healthcare, cybersecurity, and emergency response. He received his PhD in Human-Centered Computing from Clemson University in 2023 under advisor Nathan McNeese, and his BS in Psychology (Summa Cum Laude) from Clemson in 2018.

Prospective Students

Join the ARCS Lab

We are actively recruiting motivated PhD students interested in human-AI teaming, human factors, HCI, and the design of trustworthy AI systems. We welcome students from diverse backgrounds including psychology, computer science, industrial engineering, and cognitive science.

Strong Candidates Have
Background in human factors, HCI, cognitive science, psychology, or CS. Experience with experimental methods or programming. Strong written and verbal communication skills.
Research Opportunities
ARL-funded projects on human-AI teaming, trust calibration, shared mental models, and compromised AI detection. Co-authorship on high-impact publications.
